About

Silver Thread Winegarden

Silver Thread Vineyard has been one of the Finger Lakes' most quietly celebrated wineries since 1982 — a pioneer in dry wine production and organic farming on the eastern shore of Seneca Lake. In early 2025, the winery opened its new primary tasting location right on Route 414: the Silver Thread Winegarden, housed in the former Dano's Heuriger restaurant space at 9564 NY-414 in Lodi.

The Winegarden brings Silver Thread to a wider audience without sacrificing what makes it special. Tastings are paired with a locally sourced small plates menu — cheese boards, salads, sausages, and European-style spreads inspired by the space's Dano's heritage. The outdoor tent seating area offers shaded Seneca Lake views, and the welcoming, educational atmosphere reflects the Brocks' deep commitment to wine knowledge and hospitality.

Winemaker and co-owner Paul Brock is an assistant professor of Viticulture and Wine Technology at Finger Lakes Community College and a leading voice in biointensive farming in the region. Co-owner Shannon Brock is a certified wine educator and WSET Diploma holder. Together they craft small-batch, terroir-driven wines that are among the most respected in the Finger Lakes — and now more accessible than ever.

The Wines

What's Pouring

All wines are estate-grown on Silver Thread's sustainably farmed Seneca Lake vineyard — small-batch, low-intervention, and built around freshness, minerality, and site expression.

  • Riesling ★Dry to off-dry — zesty, floral, and saline with a long finish. The signature Silver Thread style.
  • Pétillant NaturelEffervescent and food-friendly — pet nat made from estate fruit with signature Finger Lakes character.
  • ChardonnayCool-climate Chardonnay with orchard fruit and bright acidity — no heavy oak treatment.
  • GewurztraminerAromatic and expressive — one of the more distinctive whites in the lineup.
  • Dry RoséEstate-grown rosé with strawberries and minerality.
  • Red BlendsEstate red from Seneca Lake fruit — bright, herbaceous, and age-worthy.

Current wine list at silverthreadwine.com — follow @silverthreadwine for updates.

Tips for Visitors

  • Start with the Riesling — it's the flagship and one of the best expressions of Seneca Lake fruit you'll find anywhere.
  • Order food alongside the tasting — the small plates are genuinely excellent and designed to pair with the wines.
  • Groups of 6 or fewer are walk-in only. Groups of 7–20 should reserve at least 7 days in advance.
  • Visit the Estate on Caywood Road by appointment (May–Oct) for a vineyard tour and a behind-the-scenes look at biointensive farming.
  • Ask about the Finger Lakes Biointensive Working Group — Paul is the founder and loves to talk about regenerative viticulture.
  • Easy to find right on Route 414 — far more accessible than the original Caywood Road location.
